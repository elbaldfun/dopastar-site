DopaStar Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 Applies to: the DopaStar app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and the DopaStar WeChat Mini Program Operator: JinXing Fang Contact: elbald.fun@gmail.com

In short

No account, no ads, no tracking. Learning records and children's names stay on your device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Records on your device

DopaStar works offline. The following are kept only in the app's own storage on your device (a private database in the app; WeChat's local storage in the Mini Program): · each child's name and avatar; · answers, mistakes, skill progress, stars, trophies, outfits, daily quests and streaks, practice dates and unfinished rounds; · settings such as grade, curriculum, questions per round and timer display, plus family-wide settings such as interface language and sound; · usage records: when the app is opened and how long it is used, summed up per day. They are used to build questions, review mistakes, show progress and resume practice, and are not uploaded by the app to the operator or any third-party server. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and no cloud sync, and does not use the camera, photos, microphone, contacts or location. Text is written to the clipboard only when you tap a "Copy" button (for example, a check report shown on screen); the app never reads the clipboard.

More than one child

You can add up to 3 children on a device (the same in the app and the Mini Program); each child's progress is kept separately. Names are typed in the app by a parent or child, are used only to show and tell children apart in the app, and stay on this device. We suggest a nickname rather than a full real name. Avatars are chosen from pictures built into the app. Deleting a child in the parent area also deletes all of that child's records on this device, and cannot be undone.

WeChat Mini Program

The Mini Program needs no sign-in and does not obtain your WeChat avatar, nickname, phone number or location. Records are kept only in WeChat's local storage. The Mini Program does not offer purchases at present. If WeChat Pay is added later, purchases would need WeChat to identify the user and a server to record what was bought; this policy and the Mini Program's privacy guidelines will be updated before that goes live. Deleting the Mini Program or clearing WeChat's storage deletes these records.

Deleting data and system backups

In the parent area you can delete a single child, or choose "Delete all data" and confirm to clear every learning record on this device. These actions cannot be undone. Your device may back up and restore data according to its settings (for example, iCloud Backup); system backups and crash diagnostics are governed by the platform's rules. The app has no cloud backup or cross-device sync of its own.

Children

The app is meant for primary-school children, ideally with a parent's guidance. The operator does not collect children's names, contact details or learning records, and the app has no ads, chat or public sharing. The app currently has no paid content and no in-app purchases. Parents can manage settings, children and records in the parent area. When contacting support, please have a parent send the email and avoid including a child's name, photos, identity information or passwords.

Email support

If you email support, the operator receives your email address, your description of the problem and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them only to answer you. The support mailbox uses Gmail, whose own rules govern its data processing. Emails are kept only while the issue is being resolved and for any necessary follow-up; you can ask the support address to access or delete emails about your request.

Changes

If the way data is handled changes, this policy and the effective date above will be updated. For privacy questions, contact elbald.fun@gmail.com. If the language versions differ, the Simplified Chinese version prevails.
